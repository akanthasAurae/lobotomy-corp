<h2 class="team-disc">Clashing</h2>

Every agent has the ability to <span class="icon clash">clash</span> once in their turn, using their respective rolls to clash against the <span class="icon abno">abnormality's</span> roll. If the agent is successful, the <span class="icon abno">abnormality's</span> attack will get cancelled and they will take damage. The same goes for the agent if they lose the <span class="icon clash">clash</span>. The winner of the <span class="icon clash">clash</span>  heals 10% of their max. <span class="icon sp">SP</span> and the loser loses 5% of their max. <span class="icon sp">SP</span>

<span class="icon abno">Abnormalities</span> instead take <span class="icon warn1">Stagger Damage</span>. After winning enough <span class="icon clash">clashes</span> against an <span class="icon abno">abnormality</span> in a short amount of time, they will become <span class="icon warn2">Staggered</span>. During the turn of the Stagger and the following turn, the abnormality's resistances will decrease by 0.5. If there are two or less Agents in a fight, the Stagger will instead become a Stance Break. 

During a Stance Break, the fastest employee can perform a Critical Strike that bypasses resistances (unless immune) and deals +1200% damage based on the E.G.O weapon's total damage.
