<h2 class="team-disc">Clashing</h2>

Every agent has the ability to <span class="icon clash">clash</span> once in their turn, using their respective rolls to <span class="icon clash">clash</span> against the <span class="icon abno">abnormality's</span> roll. If the agent is successful, the <span class="icon abno">abnormality's</span> attack will get cancelled and they will take damage. The same goes for the agent if they lose the <span class="icon clash">clash</span>. The winner of the <span class="icon clash">clash</span>  heals 10% of their max. <span class="icon sp">SP</span> and the loser loses 5% of their max. <span class="icon sp">SP</span>

<span class="icon abno">Abnormalities</span> instead take <span class="icon warn1">Stagger Damage</span>. After winning enough <span class="icon clash">clashes</span> against an <span class="icon abno">abnormality</span> in a short amount of time, they will become <span class="icon warn2">Staggered</span>. During the turn of the <span class="icon warn2">Stagger</span> and the following turn, the <span class="icon abno">abnormality's</span> resistances will decrease by 0.5. If there are two or less Agents in a fight, the <span class="icon warn2">Stagger</span> will instead become a <span class="icon warn3">Stance Break</span>. 

During a <span class="icon warn3">Stance Break</span>, the fastest employee can perform a <span class="icon warn3">Critical Strike</span> that bypasses resistances (unless immune) and deals <span class="no-icon warn3">+1200%</span> damage based on the <span class="icon weap">E.G.O weapon's</span> total damage.

In the following I will be presenting the:
> [!disc] Clashing Formatting:
> The correct formatting to rolling is as follows: 
> <code>d20 ir5 ! Attack > Target</code>
> d20 is the ceiling of the roll and ir5 is the base of the attack, which results in 5\~20. You can add modifiers into the rolls by simply adding a + or - next to the d20. You can also run the command so it rolls the same thing multiple times by adding a number before the d20, for example: <code>5d20+5 ir5 ! Attack > Target</code>
> This means that it will roll 5\~20 + 5 five times. The exclamation mark is required in order to add further text.

<h2 class="team-disc">E.G.O Abilities</h2>
 Every <span class="icon weap">E.G.O Weapon</span> has a designated skill with its own damage value, mostly being the default skill. Some however have the ability to gain different skills with different values and effects, these are gained via <span class="icon gift">E.G.O Mastery</span> which is gained the longer an agent uses an <span class="icon weap">E.G.O Weapon</span> (7+ days). Each Skill takes different amounts of <span class="icon warn1">Actions</span> and can have their own conditions to activate.
