<h1 class="team-train has-subtitle">Experience Guide</h2>

 Document ID:  #WRITER-CONTENT #TRAINING-DEPARTMENT 

> [!train] Explanation & Modifiers:
> Experience is a value of the Agents which help them increase their stats. The experience will affect how much the Agents stat levels will increase at the end of the Management Phase.
> The experience gained is calculated in the following: 
> PE-Boxes acquired * Damage Modifier * Level Modifier * Training Modifier 
> 
> This modifier is decided on how much HP% you have remaining since beginning the work, which is calculated below:
> 
> | Remaining % | Modifier |
> | :-------------: | :----------: |
> | ≥ 90%           |       0.4       |
> | ≥ 80%           |       0.6       |
> | ≥ 70%           |       0.8       |
> | > 20%           |        1         |
> | > 10%           |       1.3       |
> | ≤ 10%           |       1.5       |
> 
> Next is the Level Modifier which will be shown below:
>
> | Stat Level | Abnormality Level                                                                      
> | :-------------: | :----------: | :----------: | :----------: | :----------: | :----------: |
> | ≥ Lv. 1           |       0.4       |                 |                  |                   |                 |
> | ≥ Lv. 2           |       0.6       |
> | ≥ Lv. 3           |       0.8       |
> | > Lv. 4           |        1         |
> | > Lv. 5           |       1.3       |
