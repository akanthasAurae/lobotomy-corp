These Light Seedlings can be harvested and extracted using advanced technology to artificially bring out the E.G.O trapped within the Seedling, allowing individuals to use this E.G.O as their own, akin to Lobotomy E.G.O.

Following the topic of Seedlings and Golden Boughs: Golden Boughs were born from the shattered remnants of Lobotomy Corporation's core Singularity after the abrupt interruption of the Seed of Light Project. When the release sequence was halted at approximately ninety percent completion, every Lobotomy Corporation branch across the City entered an immediate automated lockdown, sealing both employees and Abnormalities deep beneath the earth.

These facilities were never independent systems. Each branch was directly tethered to the headquarters, acting as an extension or seedling of the central Seed of Light Project. Their local Singularity cores functioned as sprouts of the original system, continuously receiving and regulating Light generated by the headquarters. When the six-day emission was prematurely interrupted, an immense volume of unreleased, highly volatile Light remained trapped within these isolated cores. The residual Light then fused with the facilities' neural infrastructure, Enkephalin production systems, and Singularity technology. After being left buried beneath the City for extended amounts of time, this unstable concentration of Light would slowly mature into radiant, tree-like branches known as Golden Boughs.

The facilities' themselves became large containment vaults, though. Their architecture, energy conduits and suppression systems unintendedly imprisoned the Golden Bough's reality altering influence. This containment, though, was dependent on the branch remaining sealed.

The moment a Golden Bough is extracted or the underground facility housing it is breached, the containment field collapses. Freed from the structural suppression of the branch, the Bough begins releasing the immense quantity of incomplete Light still stored within it. Its influence rapidly expands beyond the facility, projecting its reality-warping properties into the surrounding District.

Unlike the original Seed of Light, which sought to resonate with all of humanity, a Golden Bough lacks both direction and a conscious inheritor. Instead, it instinctively synchronizes with the minds of those within its radius. The fears, desires, memories, and collective unconscious of the local population become the blueprint through which the Light reshapes reality.

Entire city blocks or, in the case of particularly mature Boughs, large portions of a District may become enveloped by this phenomenon. Streets twist into labyrinths, natural laws become inconsistent and manifestations of the District's collective psyche begin to physically exist. These large-scale distortions are commonly referred to as Mirror Dungeons or Phenomena Zones.
