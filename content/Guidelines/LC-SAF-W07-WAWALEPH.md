> [!safety] Purpose
> To establish mandatory safety standards for all employees assigned to perform work with <span class="icon waw">WAW</span> and <span class="icon aleph">ALEPH</span> class <span class="icon abno">Abnormalities</span>. Failure to comply with these requirements significantly increases the probability of employee <span class="icon dead">fatality</span>, <span class="icon panic">panic</span>, or <span class="icon meltdown">containment failure</span>.

<h2 class="team-safety">AUTHORIZATION REQUIREMENTS</h2>
Employees assigned to <span class="icon warn3">high-risk</span> containment must meet the following minimum standards unless otherwise authorized by a <span class="icon gift">Department Captain</span> or the <span class="no-icon team-arch.">Manager.</span>

> [!safety] WAW-Class Abnormalities
> - <span class="icon temp">Temperance</span> Lv. 4 or higher.
> - <span class="icon suit">E.G.O Suit</span> with appropriate resistances for the <span class="icon abno">Abnormality</span>.
> - Prior briefing on the <span class="icon abno">Abnormality</span> with the <span class="icon gift">Team Captain</span>
> - Announcing the Work Process to <span class="icon team-control">Control Team</span> or <span class="icon team-cmd">Central Command Team</span>

> [!safety] ALEPH-Class Abnormalities
> - <span class="icon temp">Temperance</span> Lv. 5.
>- High-grade <span class="icon suit">E.G.O Suit</span> with favourable resistances. (preferably <span class="icon waw">WAW</span>)
> - Approval from a <span class="icon gift">Team Captain</span> or Sephirot
> - Backup suppression team placed on standby before work begins.
> - (Unfortunately we lack information to make a proper safety measure against ALEPH abnormalities - Yui)

> [!safety] E.G.O Equipment
> Employees are expected to review the Abnormality's damage profile before entering containment, however personnel are forbidden from working if the E.G.O has:
> - <span class="no-icon vulnerable">Vulnerable</span> or <span class="no-icon weak">Weak</span> resistance to the <span class="icon abno">Abnormality's</span> primary damage type.
> - Severe mental instability from synchronisation
> - Damaged or compromised E.G.O

> [!safety] During Work
> Employees are obligated to read [[LC-PRT-E09-HEPLUS]] for further advice on what to do in worst case scenarios. The Safeguard shouldn't be relied on and one should work as if it didn't exist.


<h2 class="team-safety">GENERAL SAFETY NOTES</h2>

- Success rates do not guarantee employee safety.
- Higher Temperance greatly improves consistency when handling dangerous Abnormalities.
- Never rely solely on E.G.O equipment to compensate for insufficient training or experience.
- A living employee can always attempt another work assignment. A deceased employee cannot.
