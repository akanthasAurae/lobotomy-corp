Ppodae is a small, white pug puppy with "puppy dog eyes" and a curled tail.

When breaching, it changes its form to a larger, more muscular dog and grows to about 4 times its previous size. Its tail and legs grow larger, and it has a muscular torso and a blue, circular tattoo on its shoulder.
