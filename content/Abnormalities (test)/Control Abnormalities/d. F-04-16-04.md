Four-hundred Roses has the appearance of a giant rose. The Abnormality possesses a pair of thin, crooked arms covered in thorns, almost as long as its stem (also thin, crooked, and thorns-covered). Numerous beady red eyes can be seen in the crevices between the large, dark petals of the flower, which is crowned with wilted, thorny sepals. A mouth rests in the center of the flower, lined with sharp teeth. A spring of blood can be seen dripping from the mouth.

<h2 class="qliphoth has-subtitle">Breaching Information</h2>

> [!default|subtitle]
> >[!content]
> > <span class="icon abno">Core HP</span>: 1500
> > <span class="icon hp">Head HP</span>: 500
> > <span class="icon spd">SPD:</span> 1~4
> > | Breach Resistances | <span class="icon red">RED</span> | <span class="icon white">WHITE</span> | <span class="icon black">BLACK</span> | <span class="icon pale">PALE</span> |
> > | --- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
> > |  | <span class="no-icon resistant">Resistant (0.4)</span> | <span class="no-icon weak">Weak (1.2)</span> | <span class="no-icon endured">Endured (0.8)</span> | <span class="no-icon weak">Weak (1.5)</span> |

When the <span class="icon qliphoth">Qliphoth Counter</span> drops to 0, it does not physically escape, rather it grows out its roots through the department. During this phase, it cannot be targeted or hurt in anyway and will resort to using <span class="icon warn1">'Brambles that Constrict'</span>, which has the thorned roots and vines violently stab and wrap themselves around Clerks or Employees in order to drain their blood.

After getting enough <span class="icon warn3">Nourishment</span>, it finally gains the strength to dig through its containment unit, rising up in the main room of the department it resided in and releasing a low red mist that quickly fills the area. The mist is heavy, carrying the sharp scent of blood. If there are any Clerks or Employees in the main room, Four-hundred Roses will focus on them while using <span class="icon warn1">'Wound'</span> and <span class="icon warn2">'Polydipsic Petals'</span>. If there are no Clerks or Employees in the main room, it will instead search for them through the department, striking with <span class="icon warn1">'Brambles that Constrict'</span> until another Clerk or Employee shows up in the main room. 

At first, its behaviour is focused on drawing blood. It uses fast, wide-reaching root attacks to hit anything it can sense, trying to spill as much blood as possible. Every so often, it will stop and attempt to feed by lowering its roots into the pools of blood on the ground. If it successfully absorbs the blood, its behavior becomes slower and less aggressive, but it becomes tougher to damage as its resistances increase. If its feeding is interrupted—either by cleaning the blood or breaking the feeding roots—it grows more aggressive, attacking more frequently and with greater strength, though its defenses weaken as its resistances decrease.

When its health drops below a certain point, it will enter a second, much more deadly state. Blood will begin to swirl constantly around it, and it will try to absorb it to heal over time. If it is left alone during this state, it will slowly recover and eventually return to its first phase, repeating the cycle again. If the department is cleared of all agents and clerks, the Abnormality will disappear, only to reappear in another department, where the same process will begin once more.
