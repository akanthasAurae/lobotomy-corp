Four-hundred Roses has the appearance of a giant rose. The Abnormality possesses a pair of thin, crooked arms covered in thorns, almost as long as its stem (also thin, crooked, and thorns-covered). Numerous beady red eyes can be seen in the crevices between the large, dark petals of the flower, which is crowned with wilted, thorny sepals. A mouth rests in the center of the flower, lined with sharp teeth. A spring of blood can be seen dripping from the mouth.

<h2 class="">Managerial Tips</h2>

> [!qliphoth] Managerial Tips 01
> When performing <span class="icon inst">Instinct Work</span>, the Employee fed the abnormality a moderate amount of blood which boosted the amount of <span class="icon pe">PE</span> boxes obtained
>-<code><span class="icon abno">Four-hundred Roses</span> deals +50% <span class="icon weap">damage</span> and total <span class="icon pe">PE</span> produced is boosted by +50%</code>

> [!qliphoth] Managerial Tips 02
>When an Employee with Lv. 3 or less <span class="icon fort">Fortitude</span> entered the containment unit, <span class="icon abno">Four-hundred Roses</span> instantly <span class="no-icon dead">killed</span> them and immediately produced <span class="icon pe">44 PE</span> boxes. Afterwards the <span class="icon qliphoth">Qliphoth Counter</span> increased by 1
><code>- If an Agent died while working, the Work Result would be set as <span class="icon pos">Good</span> and all the gathered <span class="icon pe">PE</span> would be increased by 75%</code>

> [!qliphoth] Managerial Tips 03
> <span class="icon atta">Attachment Work</span> was replaced with <span class="icon sacr">Sacrifice Work</span> due to the <span class="icon abno">abnormality's</span> inability to emotionally attach itself to other beings. When performing this work, the Employee must enter and surrender themselves so that the abnormality may feed. It is to note that it is recommended for Employees with <span class="icon fort">high Fortitude</span> to perform this type of work.
><code>- Beginning the work immediately increases the <span class="icon qliphoth">Qliphoth Counter</span> by 1</code>
><code>- If the Employee takes 60 <span class="icon red">RED</span> damage post-resistances, the <span class="icon qliphoth">Qliphoth Counter</span> will increase by an additional 1</code>
><code>- If the employee takes 80+ <span class="icon red">RED</span> damage post-resistances, the next two works with this <span class="icon abno">Four-hundred Roses</span> will have +20% <span class="icon wrkscc">Work Success Rate</span> and it will deal 2~4 less <span class="icon red">RED</span> damage</code>
><code> - This effect will expire on its own after two <span class="icon meltdown">Qliphoth Meltdowns</span> if the <span class="icon abno">Abnormality</span> wasn't worked on.</code>

> [!qliphoth] Managerial Tips 04
> If the abnormality wasn’t worked with ‘Sacrifice’ after a while, the Qliphoth Counter lowered by 1. The lower the abnormality’s Qliphoth Counter was, the more damage it dealt.

> [!qliphoth] Managerial Tips 05
> Attachment Work has been replaced with a special ‘Sacrifice’ Work Type due to the Abnormality’s inability to emotionally attach itself to other beings. If this Work Type is used, the Agent that performed said Work would lose a heavy amount of HP, increasing the Abnormality’s Qliphoth Counter by 2, raising Work Success Rate by 20% and lowering the Abnormality’s damage for the next two works.

> [!qliphoth] Managerial Tips 06
> If an Agent performs ‘Sacrifice’ more than once in a day, they will be killed and the Qliphoth Counter will drop to 0.
