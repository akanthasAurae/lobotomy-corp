<h1 class="teth icon faux has-subtitle">Punishing Bird</h2>

> [!teth|subtitle faux]
> > [!content]
> > Maximum <span class="icon pe">PE</span> Boxes: 12
> > <span class="icon qliphoth">Maximum Qliphoth Counter:</span> 4
> > Work Hazard Type: <span class="icon red">RED</span> (2~4)
> > Document ID:  #CONTROL-CONTAINMENT #O-02-56
> > Observation Lv. 4

Punishing Bird is a small, white-feathered bird with small wings and a red mark on its chest. While in its containment room, it rests on a black perch. Punishing Bird becomes entirely red while being suppressed.
