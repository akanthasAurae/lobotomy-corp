<h1 class="team-train has-subtitle">Training Team</h2>

> [!train|subtitle]
> > [!content]
> > The Training team is in charge of composing and regulating company policies and various management procedures. They also run general-purpose training programs to help employees adapt well to their new departments.

> [!train] Continuous Service Benefits:
> Increases the Growth Rate of the Department’s Agents.
> Lv 1: +5% (1\~2 Days of Service)
> Lv 2: +10% (3\~6 Days of Service)
> Lv 3: +15% (7+ Days of Service)

<h2 class="team-train">Training Team Missions</h2>

#### 1. I Want to Help:
- [ ] Promote two Agents to a higher level

#### 2. Unwanted Kindness:
- [ ] Promote an Agent to Level 4 or higher

#### 3. I Just Want to Be a Good Person:
- [ ] Suppress 4 different <span class="icon abno">Abnormalities</span>

#### 4. Practical Training:
- [ ] Complete the day with 5 or more Level 5 employees deployed

#### 5. Suppress the Core of Hod:
- [ ] Requires <span class="no-icon team-control">Malkuth</span>, <span class="no-icon team-info">Yesod</span> and <span class="no-icon team-cmd">Tiphereth</span>'s missions completed

<h2 class="team-train">Training Team Research</h2>

> [!train|0.5] 1. Supply Educational Manual (Unknown)
> Educational Manuals will be supplied to help our employees learn faster.
> <code>- All employees gain +50% EXP from work and suppressions</code>

> [!train|0.5] 2. Veteran Resolve (Unknown)
> Employees who achieve exceptional proficiency serve as examples to others, inspiring confidence throughout the facility.
> <code>- Level 4 and 5 employees become immune to <span class="icon panic">panic</span> from Clerks dying</code>

> [!train|0.5] 3. Suppression Doctrine (Unknown)
> The successful suppression of multiple Abnormalities has established standardized combat doctrine, improving every employee's ability to respond to breaches.
> <code>- All employees deal +15% damage against breaching <span class="icon abno">Abnormalities</span></code>

> [!train|0.5] 4. Elite Operations (Unknown)
> An experienced workforce is capable of maintaining exceptional operational standards, even under extreme conditions.
> <code>- All Level 5 employees gain +10% <span class="icon wrkscc">Work Success Rate</span> and deal +10% damage to <span class="icon abno">Abnormalities</span></code>
