<h1 class="team-safety has-subtitle">Safety Team</h2>

> [!safety|subtitle]
> > [!content]
> > The Safety Team gives safety training to new employees and develops action plans for all kinds of potential emergencies that can occur in the company. They establish strategies for situations such as escaping Abnormalities, panicking employees and security breaches, as well as setting up safety guidelines for others to follow. They are notably meticulous and punctual.

<h2 class="team-safety">Safety Team Research</h2>

> [!safety] 1. Emergency Safety Protocol (Researched)
> With fewer employees available to respond to workplace incidents, a reliable emergency system is required to prevent avoidable losses. The Safeguard system has been developed as a final measure to protect employees from fatal accidents during containment procedures.
> <code>- When an employee would receive fatal damage during a Work Procedure, it prevents that damage and immediately removes the employee from the Containment Unit, then causes a <span class="icon qliphoth">Qliphoth Malfunction</span> in the department decreasing <span class="icon wrkscc">Work Success Rate</span> by 20% and increasing <span class="icon abno">Abnormality</span> damage by 20% which can last up to two <span class="icon qliphoth">Qliphoth Meltdowns</span></code>
> <code>- Consumes 20% of the facility's total <span class="icon pe">Energy</span></code>
> <code>- Can activate up to 2 times per day</code>

> [!safety] 2. Physical and Mental Recovery Protocol (Researched)
> Analysis of employee injury and psychological damage has allowed the Safety Department to improve recovery procedures and reduce the long-term effects of physical and psychological trauma.
> <code>- Successfully completing a work procedure <span class="icon regen">heals</span> the employee for 15% of their maximum <span class="icon hp">HP</span> & <span class="icon sp">SP</span></code>

> [!safety|0.5] 3. Hazard Prevention Training (Unknown)
> By studying incidents where employees were pushed to their limits, new safety procedures have been created to prevent critical failures.
> <code>- Employees below 25% <span class="icon hp">HP</span> or <span class="icon sp">SP</span> gain +0.2 resistance to <span class="icon red">RED</span> and <span class="icon white">WHITE</span> damage respectively, as well as +0.1 resistance against <span class="icon black">BLACK</span> damage</code>

> [!safety|0.5] 4. Advanced Safeguard System (Unknown)
> The Safeguard system has been improved to provide more reliable emergency intervention.
> <code>- When activated, the Safeguard prevents fatal damage and restores the employee to 25% <span class="icon hp">HP</span> and 25% <span class="icon sp">SP</span> instead of only stopping the damage, then damage taken is reduced by 50% for 10 seconds (1 turn)</code>
> <code>- If activated within a work procedure, the employee will not be kicked out and instead will have the choice to leave or finish the work. Finishing the work will not trigger any <span class="icon qliphoth">Qliphoth</span> penalty.</code>
> <code>- If activated within a Battle, it won't trigger any <span class="icon qliphoth">Qliphoth</span> penalty, then can't be activated again for the rest of the battle. (once per battle)</code>
> <code>- <span class="icon pe">Energy</span> penalty will still apply</code>
> <code>- Can be activated 3 times per day</code>
