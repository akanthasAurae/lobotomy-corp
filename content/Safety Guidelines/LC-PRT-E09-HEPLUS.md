> [!safety] EMERGENCY PHONE PROTOCOL & PURPOSE
> This protocol has been written to establish a standardized procedure for agents to safely utilize the <span class="icon warn1">Emergency Phone</span> installed in <span class="icon he">HE+</span> abnormality containment units (<span class="icon he">HE</span>, <span class="icon waw">WAW</span>, and <span class="icon aleph">ALEPH</span> risk levels) during containment threats, agent endangerment, or unexpected anomaly escalation.

<h2 class="team-safety">DEVICE OVERVIEW</h2>
The <span class="icon warn1">Emergency Phone</span> (Model 9A-R) is located inside containment units, mounted to the right or left of the unit entrance. It is designated by a red receiver icon and a yellow caution border. When lifted, the handset opens a direct channel to <span class="icon team-control">Control</span> or <span class="icon team-cmd">Central Command</span> in <span class="no-icon asiyah">Asiyah</span> and <span class="no-icon briah">Briah</span> respectively. Both Departments may pick up if the emergency is within <span class="no-icon atziluth">Atziluth</span>.

Use is limited to <span class="icon warn2">Emergency Circumstances</span> only.

<h2 class="team-safety">AUTHORIZED USE CASES</h2>
Agents are permitted to activate the <span class="icon warn1">Emergency Phone</span> only when one or more of the following are observed:

> [!safety] 1. Abnormality Behaviour Deviation
> - Sudden movement, shape change, or vocalization not documented in prior logs.
> - Interruption or reversal of established work response cycles.

> [!safety] 2. Breach Warning / Containment Failure Signs
> - <span class="icon qliphoth">Qliphoth</span> counter shows unexpected drop within a work process.
> - Door locking mechanisms fail or flicker.

> [!safety] 3. Agent Emergency
> - <span class="icon hp">HP</span> drops into critical zone.
> - <span class="icon panic">Panic</span> imminent or underway.
> - Visual/mental instability reported during observation (e.g., hallucinations, voices, time dilation).

> [!safety] 4 Command Silence / Data Blackout
> - Agent receives no feedback or observation for 10 seconds or more during an active work order.
> - This is only applied if the Agent working is actively observed by their respective <span class="icon gift">Team Captain</span>

<h2 class="team-safety">USAGE PROCEDURE</h2>

1. Calmly approach the phone without disrupting visual or spatial triggers known to the <span class="icon abno">abnormality</span>.
2. Lift the receiver. No dialling is required.
3. Clearly state:
 “This is Agent [Name], reporting from Containment Unit [X-##]. Working on [Abnormality Name / Codename]. Emergency in progress. Requesting immediate assistance.” 
4. Remain on the line. Await further instruction. 
5. Follow all <span class="icon team-control">Control Team</span> / <span class="icon team-cmd">Central Command's</span> orders exactly. Deviation may compromise both the agent and facility integrity.

<h2 class="team-safety">RESTRICTIONS & CONSEQUENCES</h2>

> [!safety] Unauthorized use of the Emergency Phone will result in: 
>- <span class="no-icon team-disc">Disciplinary</span> Review 
>- ██████ ██████████ ████████
>- <span class="icon pe">Enkephalin</span> Consumption Increase

- The phone enters a 60-second cooldown lock after use. Repeated activation will auto-disable the device for the remainder of the shift.
- Misuse during <span class="icon aleph">ALEPH</span> operations may escalate to direct Sephirot intervention.

<h2 class="team-safety">NOTES FOR AGENTS</h2>

- This device does not calm the <span class="icon abno">abnormality</span>, halt breach progression, or affect agent <span class="icon panic">panic</span> levels. Matter of fact it may make it worse.
- The phone is a tool of communication, not protection. Its misuse endangers the shift.
